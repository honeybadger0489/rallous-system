# Magic (The Winds)

You did **not** arrive with Iron’s book equipped. The Winds are a path, not a loadout. Steel and thirst first.

The wreckage chest is bread / leather / stone. **No spellbook.** Do not craft a book just to tick a boot list.

## Four rumours

Walk to them. Quest chapter **The Winds**.

| Path | Tone | Score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Colleges** | Altdorf rumour: lecterns, licensed fire, holy light | `rallous.magic` 1 |
| **Ice** | A court, not a novelty. Reach cold. | 2 |
| **Death** | Grave-talk. A priest who does not smile. | 3 |
| **Blood** | A rite, a count’s cellar, a wheel that smells of iron | 4 |

Spell wheel (**R**, Iron’s) exists when you have **earned** a book. Until then **R** is Recruits Host Command.

## Engine

Iron’s Spells ’n Spellbooks is in the pack. We did not add a starter kit. Colleges / Ice / death / Blood are rumours until you walk to them.

Malum is slower occult if you want it later. Not required for the first hour.
