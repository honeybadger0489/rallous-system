# Crash and death

A new world is a **Warp-crash**. You are not a tourist at a Reikland court.

## Wake

Look down. Bowl of blackstone / crying obsidian, campfire, wreckage chest (**Warp-tainted relic** / echo shard — **no** Iron’s spellbook). Title says Warp-crash. World spawn is the crater. Your spawnpoint is **your** crater.

Walk to the nearest bannered camp. That named lord is first contact. See [Factions](Factions.md).

A later HQ hook plants a white banner + fence at the crater (`rallous_crater_hq`). That is a DLC marker, not a faction capital.

## Friends

A second player who joins **this world** crashes **somewhere else** — their own crater, not yours. Up to 12 far slots.

Solo demo (cheats ON): `/function rallous_old_world:crash/demo_friend_elsewhere`

You do not share a spawn circle. Meet on the road.

## Death

| You had… | You come back… |
| --- | --- |
| No civ-bed | **Your** crater. Same player. No second crash. |
| A **claimed camp** bed (lord / camp marker nearby) or a **join** civ-bed | That bed. |
| A wilderness bed | The crater. Wilderness beds do not stick. |

Sleep at a **claimed camp** (lord / camp marker nearby) or after **join**. A mute vanilla villager does not make a wilderness bed stick. Dying does not scatter you to a new Warp-hole.

Force a return: `/function rallous_old_world:crash/return_crater`

## What this is not

- No six named lords at spawn (that was 0.2.2 court; `/function rallous_old_world:summon_lords` is a refuse line).
- No starter college letter.
- Death is not a new crash. You already landed.
