# Inspiration — Rallous × the island

These are **original** loops. They steal *feel* from Warhammer and ARK, not names, chapters, or Studio Wildcard species. If a line sounds too close to a trademark, rename it before it leaves this folder. Legal backbone: [LEGAL-NOTES.md](LEGAL-NOTES.md).

Working names used below: **Rallous** (the dying human polity), **the Black Tide** (corruption), **Ash-Wights** (lost kin), **Crown-Beasts** (apex fauna), **the Island** (your map).

---

## 1. The first Crown-Beast

You do not spawn with a mount. You track a wounded **Crown-Beast** (Fossils rex / Cataclysm leftover — pick one analogue), starve it, fence it, and imprint the hatchling. The adult is a siege weapon: it tanks Born in Chaos night raids while your tribe finishes a gate. Failure state: it imprints on nobody and eats the handler.

**ASA later:** knockout torpor + imprint timer + “siege saddle” engram.

## 2. Chapter as tribe, not costume

A party in Open Parties and Claims **is** a chapter-analogue: banner, claim color, kill-on-sight toward rivals. Progression is gear and beasts, not a skill tree named after the Imperium. Rank titles are yours: *Keeper, Ash-Knight, Void-Warden*.

**ASA later:** tribe ranks + cosmetic banners; no GW chapter names.

## 3. Black Tide nights

Enhanced Celestials blood moons are retconned as **Tidefall**. Spawn tables spike (Born in Chaos). LSO thirst ticks faster (config). Malum spirits get cheaper to reap and more dangerous to hold. The tribe either bunkers in gothic brick or sorties on tamed beasts.

**ASA later:** world event with a corruption meter, not a named Chaos God.

## 4. Hive-ruin as map gen

When Dungeons Arise + YUNG’s + Towns and Towers are **dead hive-cities**. You strip brass (Create), stained glass (Chipped), and relic weapons (Simply Swords) while Cataclysm bosses sleep in the crypt. Terralith peaks are orbital-plateau analogues — still just mountains.

## 5. Dinosaur cavalry + loud guns

TaCZ with **original** heavy rifles. Epic Fight for the boarding axe. The fantasy is “I am too small for this animal and too loud for this chapel,” not a licensed bolter. Magistu gothic plate is the silhouette; rename sets in-game via anvil / resource pack.

## 6. Foundry vs wilderness (two tech trees)

**Wild path:** Fossils, Farmer’s Delight, Hardcore Torches (optional), LSO camping.  
**Foundry path:** Create mill → steam (later Steam ’n Rails) → Big Cannons (later).  
Tribes specialize. A foundry tribe without beasts loses the night. A beast tribe without walls loses the raid.

**ASA later:** engrams split Primitive / Husbandry / Foundry.

## 7. Alien basement

Alex’s Caves biomes are **xenos ecology**, not Tyranid IP. Magnetic caves = dead ship guts. Toxic caves = spore sea. You tame *nothing* there until you bring a Crown-Beast that can breathe it. Carry On exists so you can drag a crate of samples back to the claim.

## 8. The Leviathan contract

Cataclysm’s sea/earth titans are **hireable only after you survive them**. First kill is a raid boss. Second interaction (datapack later) could be a “scar tribute” tame via Salvation & Sacrifice. Until then, they are weather.

## 9. Ash-Wight corruption as a status, not a faction

Don’t spawn “Chaos Marines.” Spawn **Ash-Wights**: players or villagers who stayed out in Tidefall too long (Born in Chaos skins + Malum effects). Curing is expensive (Farmer’s Delight feast + Malum rite). PvP tribes can *weaponize* infection by camping rivals outside walls.

## 10. Drop-chapel logistics

Immersive Aircraft (later) is a clumsy dropship. Early game: beast caravans + claimed roads (Macaw’s Bridges over Terralith rivers). Waystones stay out of v0.1 on purpose — Ark doesn’t hand you fast travel.

## 11. The Emperor is a furnace

Create’s blaze burners / LSO heat sources become **shrine-furnaces**. Roleplay only at first; later a datapack can require a lit shrine to keep Black Tide spawn rates down inside a claim. No aquila. A geometric sunburst of your own is enough.

## 12. Park that went feral

If you ever spin a **second** instance with Jurassic Revived instead of Fossils: the island is a collapsed gene-park. That is Jurassic Park, not Ark and not 40k — keep it in its own save so DNA machines don’t double-spawn with Fossils.

## 13. Void crusade (far later)

Ad Astra death-worlds as **other Rallous spheres**. Only after Overworld tribes are stable. Same guns, worse climate. Still original planet names.

## 14. Sergeant’s ledger (quests, later)

FTB Quests / Mine and Slash are *optional* and can turn this into DawnCraft. If you add them, quest text should be field orders: survey cave, return with a live egg, hold the bridge until dawn. Not “collect 10 heretic flesh.”

## 15. What winning looks like

A claimed plateau, a foundry that prints plate, a breeding pit of Crown-Beasts, a gun wall for Tidefall, and a rival tribe on the next Terralith spit. That is the prototype. If it is fun in Minecraft, it is worth rebuilding in ASA under the Rallous name.
