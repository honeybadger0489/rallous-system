# Magic (The Winds)

You did **not** arrive with Iron’s book equipped. The Winds are a path, not a loadout. Steel and thirst first.

The wreckage chest is bread / leather / stone. **No spellbook.** Do not craft a book just to tick a boot list.

## How you find it in the first hour

1. Crash. No book in the chest. No book in the hotbar.
2. Walk to the nearest bannered camp (minutes, not a teleport).
3. A **named lectern** holds a letter. Take it. That is the path.
4. Open the camp barrel. Rare: Iron’s **common ink** plus the same letter. Never a filled spellbook.
5. Dungeon / library / stronghold chests already hide Iron’s ink and scrolls (the mod). Inscribe at an inscription table.
6. Spell wheel (**R**) waits until you have **earned** a book. Until then **R** is Recruits Host Command.

Cheats: `/function rallous_winds:hint` restates this. `/function rallous_winds:place_here` plants a lectern at your feet (still no spellbook).
