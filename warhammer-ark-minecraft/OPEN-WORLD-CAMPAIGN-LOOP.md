# Open-world campaign loop — a day in the warband

This is the **default session loop**. If a play session does not look like this most of the time, the pack has slid back into sandbox or into a quest UI.

You wake up **hungry** in a camp you actually built (ARK), not in an Adventurer’s Guild instance. You water the animals, check the freeze-box, and look at the sky: Serene Seasons will tell you whether the ride will cost you calories. You saddle the horse (or the ugly early tame you stole from a plains barn) and you **ride** — walking is for the first hour of Act I only.

The atlas (Antique Atlas 4) is a blank smear except where your boots have been. A charcoal line is the coach road toward a CTOV town you can already see as smoke. On the way you can ignore everything: a Bountiful board at a roadside inn, a gathering node (heartland iron, fen peat, hold-road marble), a Recruits patrol fighting bandits. That is New World. You can also **not** ignore it, because your warband eats.

You reach the town hub and do four verbs: stash loot, sell, recruit or heal one soldier, talk to a person with a name. If you are **committed**, you pin the main FTB Quests beat (a river fort, a warlord’s keep, a coffin that must not arrive). If you are **wandering**, you take the miller’s job and leave the elector’s captain talking to the air. The map does not freeze. The captain will still be there next week.

Sometime after noon you are in a **fight that is about a place**: a MineColonies raid on *your* palisade, a Recruits field battle on a ridge, a crypt, a Fossils apex you are not ready for. Epic Fight + Epic Knights, not a bolter. If you win, standing ticks up and maybe a banner changes. If you lose, you run. The province does not delete itself.

Before dark you try to **tame or breed something** (horse, Fossils hatchling, Alex’s Mob) because the campaign is a warband, not a cutscene. You ride home — or to a new camp if home burned. You cook, repair, sleep in a bed you placed. In the journal, a **rumor** may have unlocked: a letter about the Principalities, graffiti about the north. You do not teleport to it. Tomorrow you can follow the rumor, or go back for the miller, or push the colony one building higher. That is the whole game.
